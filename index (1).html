A mechanical instrument for the only thing that cannot be manufactured.

Chapter 01 — The billet

One piece.

The case is cut from a single billet of grade-5 titanium. Fourteen hours of machining. Ninety-one percent of it becomes swarf.

Chapter 02 — The architecture

Every part.

Two hundred and thirty-one components, stacked along one axis. Nothing decorative. Nothing hidden that didn't earn its place.

Chapter 03 — The calibre

The heart.

Calibre M-01. Twenty-eight thousand eight hundred vibrations an hour — eight beats a second, held for three days.

Chapter 04 — The assembly

Back together.

Eleven stages, one pair of hands. A single watchmaker sees each movement from plate to regulation.

Chapter 05 — After dark

It keeps going.

Indices charged with Super-LumiNova X1 hold the night for eight hours. The watch does not need you awake to keep time.

Manifesto

We did not set out to tell the time. Clocks already do that. We set out to make its passing feel like something you could hold.

A quartz movement is more accurate than anything we will ever build. We know. Accuracy was never the difficult part — permanence was.

The 01 is designed to be serviced for a hundred years. Every component is documented, tooled and stocked. Nothing in it is glued.

Origin

Three engineers,
one lathe, one room.

Madhvam began in a converted print works with a second-hand Swiss lathe and a stubborn disagreement about tolerances. We spent the first two years making nothing we were willing to sell.

The 01 is the first watch that survived our own review. It is not our best idea. It is the one we could not find a fault in.

  1. 2016

    The disagreement

    A satellite timing team leaves aerospace to find out how accurate a mechanical escapement can honestly be.

  2. 2018

    Calibre zero

    Forty-one prototype movements. Thirty-nine fail regulation. Two are kept, and neither is ever worn outside the workshop.

  3. 2021

    The billet decision

    Two-part cases are abandoned. Every 01 case will be cut from one solid piece, at four times the cost.

  4. 2024

    M-01 certified

    Chronometer results at -1/+4 seconds a day across six positions, over a 72-hour reserve.

  5. Today

    Five hundred

    Annual output is capped at the number of movements one workshop can finish by hand. It will not increase.

Craft

Three things we refused to compromise.

01 — The billet

Cut, not cast

Fourteen hours on a five-axis mill, then forty minutes of hand-brushing per case. Cast cases are cheaper and softer. We tried both.

02 — The calibre

M-01, in-house

Free-sprung balance, 27 jewels, twin barrels. Designed so a watchmaker in 2090 can service it with tools that already exist.

03 — The dial

Legible at a glance

Guilloché turned on a rose engine from 1912, then lacquered eight times. Indices are applied, never printed.
